User Guide

Temporality observes how an AI agent works: what it knew and did, where its knowledge came from, what held up — and returns verified experience to later runs. This section is a practical guide, from installation to everyday work.

Get started→

Chat and runsDialogue with the agent — from a message to a result: streaming, amendments, approvals, delegation. Agents and teamsCreating and configuring agents — models, tools, sandboxes, evolution; agent teams as pipelines.

Knowledge and experience

KnowledgeHow the agent accumulates experience — the knowledge lifecycle, the area map, dossiers, contradictions, bitemporality. Observability and storageThe dashboard, run traces, the experience timeline and the sandbox file storage. SkillsLiving skills — reusable agent procedures with a contract, versions and quality evaluations. ArtifactsA registry of reference documents — coding policies, runbooks and templates agents load on demand.

Automation

Inbound and channelsConnect Matrix, Telegram, Slack, Mattermost and webhooks — the agent receives tasks from your team's chat. TriggersStart agents on a schedule, by webhook or on system events.

Operations

Harness OperationsHow to deploy, configure and operate Temporality as a working harness — the stack, upgrades, providers, authentication, knowledge, skills, sandboxes, observability, backups and troubleshooting.

Working with the agent

Forms & approvalsHow the agent asks a human structured questions — cards in the chat, a button in your messenger, a browser form behind a one-time link — with server-side validation. FilesHow the agent hands files to a human — into the chat, into your messenger — and where to pick them up. Evolution & qualityHow agents propose changes to their own configuration, how the evaluation gate verifies the effect, how to roll a version back — and where evaluation cases come from without hand-writing them. Job titles & groupsA job title is the natural group address — invite every QA engineer to a call, or send the new instructions to all developers.
